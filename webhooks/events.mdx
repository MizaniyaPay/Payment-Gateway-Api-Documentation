---
title: "Webhook events"
description: "The events we send and what to do for each."
---

| Event | When | Final |
|---|---|---|
| `payment.succeeded` | Paid: fulfil the order. | Yes |
| `payment.failed` | Did not go through. | Yes |
| `payment.expired` | Payment window closed. | Yes |
| `payment.canceled` | Payer canceled. | Yes |
| `payment.abandoned` | Payer left the checkout. | Yes |
| `payment.processing` | Being confirmed with the bank: hold the order. | No |

<Warning>
Only `payment.succeeded` means you have been paid. On `payment.processing`, hold the order: the card may already be charged, and it always ends in `success` or `failed`.
</Warning>

<Info>
A card declined with attempts left sends **no** webhook: the payment returns to `created`. After the 3rd failed card, it becomes `failed` and one `payment.failed` is sent.
</Info>

The event name is in the `event` body field and in the `X-Mizaniya-Event` header. See the [body format](/webhooks/overview#body).

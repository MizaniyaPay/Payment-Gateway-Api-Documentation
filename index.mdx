Mizaniya Pay lets you accept **CIB** and **Edahabia** card payments in **DZD**. You call two endpoints; Mizaniya Pay handles the card, the OTP and the bank.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Card schemes | CIB, Edahabia |
| Currency | DZD |
| Checkout languages | `ar`, `fr`, `en` |
| Path prefix | `/api/v1` |
| Payment window | 15 minutes |
| Request amounts | Centimes (dinars × 100) |

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Your server sends the amount in centimes and your order reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redirect">
    Send the payer to the `checkoutUrl` you get back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payer pays">
    Card details and the OTP code from their bank, on our hosted page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the result">
    A signed webhook hits your server. You can also call `GET /payments/{id}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfil">
    Ship the order when the status is `success`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [the payment flow](/concepts/payment-flow) for the full sequence.
